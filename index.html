Coverage

All repeaters cover the I-95/I-476 corridor. The 146.685 primary repeater covers ~50 mile radius from Center City. W3PHL + W3PHL-3 are linked via autopatch during Monday Evening Net.

About PARC
Serving the Delaware Valley since 1947.
Our Mission

The Philadelphia Amateur Radio Club (PARC), operating under callsign W3PHL, promotes the art and science of amateur radio communication and experimentation. We foster emergency preparedness, technical education, and camaraderie among licensed radio amateurs in the greater Philadelphia metro area.

PARC is affiliated with the ARRL and serves as the primary ARES group for Philadelphia County under the Philadelphia Office of Emergency Management.
